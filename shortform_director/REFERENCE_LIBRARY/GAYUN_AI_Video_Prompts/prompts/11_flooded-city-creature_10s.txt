Create a 10-second cinematic animated short based on the visual world of the reference image.

FORMAT:
Vertical 9:16.
Exactly 10 seconds.
6 distinct cinematic shots with clear scene transitions.
Dreamlike narrative animation.
Integrated music and environmental sound design.
No subtitles.
No typography.
Minimal or no dialogue.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CORE STORY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

A young girl is sitting alone on a submerged pedestrian crossing
inside a completely flooded city.

The city has strangely adapted to the ocean.

Fish swim between buildings.
Sea turtles pass through intersections.
Jellyfish float beside traffic lights.

The girl seems completely accustomed to this world.

Then she notices something below her.

At first, only a shadow.

Then something impossibly enormous
slowly passes beneath the flooded street.

She realizes she is sitting directly above it.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL STYLE — CRITICAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the exact dreamy visual philosophy of the reference.

Hand-painted surreal anime-inspired editorial illustration,
but NOT conventional commercial anime.

Soft watercolor and gouache texture.
Subtle paper grain.
Delicate imperfect outlines.
Dreamlike atmospheric perspective.

Color palette:

luminous turquoise,
aquamarine,
cyan,
deep ocean blue,
pale icy blue,
soft white,
small coral-red accents.

The flooded city should feel beautiful rather than apocalyptic.

Crystal-clear blue water fills the entire urban environment.

Streetlights and traffic lights rise through the water.

White pedestrian crossing stripes appear suspended beneath the surface.

Buildings continue downward into mysterious blue depths.

Fish swim naturally through streets.

Sea turtles drift between intersections.

Jellyfish float slowly beside architecture.

Sunlight from far above creates moving caustic patterns
across walls, roads, skin, and objects.

Tiny bubbles constantly rise toward the surface.

Everything moves slowly,
as though the entire city is dreaming underwater.

Create a 10-second cinematic animated short with a clear miniature story and multiple shot transitions.

The visual style must remain consistent throughout:
hand-painted editorial illustration, expressive storybook animation, visible brush texture, slightly rough painted edges, rich cyan-blue sky, soft painterly white clouds, dark charcoal-blue pigeons with subtle teal and violet feather highlights, orange-red beaks, exaggerated expressive eyes, cinematic but clearly illustrated, NOT photorealistic, NOT 3D CGI.

The story is simple:

A person wakes up lying on the ground.

A circle of pigeons is staring down at them.

One pigeon gets uncomfortably close.

The person suddenly realizes what is happening, panics, and violently sits upright.

The sudden movement scares every pigeon into the air.

The person is left sitting alone, confused, watching them disappear.

IMPORTANT:
Use actual cinematic shot progression.
Do not keep the entire 10 seconds as one static pigeon composition.
The camera must physically change because the person suddenly sits up.

IMPORTANT ANIMATION RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain the SAME pigeons throughout the sequence.

Keep anatomy bird-like.

Pigeons should move naturally:
small neck jerks,
head tilts,
blinking,
tiny footsteps,
rapid wingbeats during escape.

Their humor comes from realistic pigeon behavior combined with exaggerated framing.

Do NOT make them talk.

Do NOT give them human facial expressions.

Do NOT turn them into cartoon mascots.

The opening pigeon circle should closely resemble an exaggerated illustrated fisheye perspective:
pigeon heads entering from all sides,
all looking downward toward the viewer,
large blue sky visible between them.

When the person sits up,
the scene must transform spatially into a believable park environment.

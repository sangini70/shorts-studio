Create a 7-second high-energy mixed-media tennis fashion film.

CORE VISUAL IDEA:

REAL PHOTOGRAPHIC TENNIS ACTION
combined with
HAND-PAINTED ILLUSTRATION.

The athlete must remain genuinely photographic:
real skin,
real fabric,
real racket,
real shoes,
real physical lighting,
real photographic body proportions.

BUT the energy created by the athlete is illustrated.

Every racket swing generates enormous hand-painted ribbons,
crayon streaks,
oil-pastel marks,
dry-brush lines,
paint splashes and graphic color fields.

The painted elements exist physically around the photographic athlete,
sometimes behind the body,
sometimes wrapping around the body,
sometimes passing in front of the camera.

The contrast between REAL PHOTOGRAPHY and RAW HAND-DRAWN PAINT
is the entire aesthetic.

Do NOT convert the athlete into an illustration.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL STYLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Mixed-media editorial sports collage.

1990s experimental sports advertising.
Vintage tennis photography.
Contemporary fashion editorial collage.
Analog magazine cutout.
Screen-printed sports photography.
Hand-painted acrylic strokes.
Oil pastel.
Wax crayon.
Dry brush.
Gouache.
Marker scribbles.

Photographic athlete cut cleanly from the environment.

Backgrounds are extremely minimal:

warm ivory,
deep charcoal,
off-white,
occasionally flat cobalt.

Photographic portions contain:

visible film grain,
slightly crushed blacks,
high-contrast flash photography,
subtle photocopy texture,
coarse newspaper grain.

Illustrated portions contain:

hot pink,
vermillion,
sunflower yellow,
cobalt blue,
electric cyan,
mint green,
violet,
coral,
lime.

Paint should NEVER look like digital neon VFX.

It must look physically handmade.

Uneven pigment.
Visible bristles.
Dry edges.
Crayon grain.
Paint thickness.
Broken lines.
Overlapping strokes.
Imperfect registration.

0:00–0:01.0 — SERVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

BLACK-AND-WHITE PHOTOGRAPHIC SHOT.

A real tennis athlete prepares to serve.

Extreme low angle.

Almost completely monochrome.

Ivory tennis court.
Charcoal background.

Camera slowly pushes toward the athlete.

The athlete throws the tennis ball upward.

The ball is the ONLY colored object:

fluorescent tennis yellow.

Everything becomes silent.

The ball rises.

Higher.

Higher.

At the peak—

CUT.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:01.0–0:01.8 — IMPACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME PHOTOGRAPHIC CLOSE-UP.

Real racket.

Real tennis ball.

Real hand.

The racket hits the ball.

THWACK!

AT THE EXACT MOMENT OF IMPACT:

an enormous hand-painted explosion erupts
from the racket.

HOT PINK.

YELLOW.

COBALT.

GREEN.

RED.

Thick paint streaks blast across frame.

The paint should look like
someone violently dragged wet acrylic
across a printed sports photograph.

Paint splatters hit the "camera."

SMASH CUT.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:01.8–0:02.8 — COLOR TRAIL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SIDE PROFILE.

The photographic athlete lands
and immediately runs forward.

Behind their body stretches
an enormous rainbow trail.

NOT a smooth rainbow.

Instead:

15–20 independent hand-painted stripes.

Different thicknesses.

Different textures.

Some oil pastel.

Some dry brush.

Some translucent marker.

Some thick acrylic.

The stripes follow the athlete's movement
like physical evidence of speed.

Camera tracks rapidly beside the player.

The photographic body remains perfectly sharp.

The painted background streaks violently.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:02.8–0:03.6 — BALL POV
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HARD CUT.

CAMERA FOLLOWS THE TENNIS BALL.

The fluorescent yellow ball flies directly through
an empty cream-colored space.

Every bounce creates a painted mark.

BOUNCE.

A cobalt paint circle explodes underneath.

BOUNCE.

Hot-pink brushstroke.

BOUNCE.

Yellow and red splatter.

The ball suddenly flies directly toward camera.

WHOOSH.

It completely fills frame.

MATCH CUT.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:03.6–0:04.5 — SECOND PLAYER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The yellow ball exits frame
and reveals another REAL PHOTOGRAPHIC TENNIS ATHLETE.

Dynamic airborne forehand.

The player jumps diagonally through
a completely empty ivory background.

Behind the body:

a gigantic diagonal wall
of hand-painted rainbow stripes.

The painted marks extend beyond the frame.

The athlete swings.

THWACK!

The racket cuts THROUGH the painted stripes.

The stripes physically split apart.

Several painted fragments peel away
like pieces of colored paper.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:04.5–0:05.3 — RAPID COLLAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

FAST RHYTHMIC MONTAGE.

0.15–0.20 second cuts.

REAL photographic shoe sliding.

SKRRRT.

Red crayon line appears beneath it.

CUT.

Real hand gripping racket.

Yellow paint scribble wraps around wrist.

CUT.

Real focused eye.

Blue brushstroke crosses behind the head.

CUT.

Tennis ball.

Pink circle hand-drawn around it.

CUT.

Racket strings flexing.

Green paint splatter.

CUT.

Photographic athlete silhouette.

Entire background suddenly becomes cobalt.

Every cut should feel like
someone rapidly rebuilding a fashion magazine collage
by hand.

0:05.3–0:06.2 — FINAL HIT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME WIDE SHOT.

Photographic athlete appears relatively small
against enormous negative space.

The ball approaches.

The player swings with full force.

THWACK!

This time the racket creates
ONE MASSIVE PAINT STROKE.

The stroke begins at the racket
and curves around the entire athlete.

It forms a huge sweeping arc:

yellow →
red →
pink →
blue →
green.

The brushstroke travels toward camera.

It becomes enormous.

Paint texture fills the entire frame.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:06.2–0:07.0 — POSTER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The moving paint abruptly FREEZES.

Music stops.

Camera pulls backward slightly.

We discover the final composition:

a completely still photographic tennis athlete
suspended in a powerful athletic pose,

surrounded by one enormous multicolored
hand-painted swoosh.

The image now looks like
a finished experimental sports magazine poster.

Photographic body.

Hand-painted energy.

Huge negative space.

Visible grain.

Visible paper texture.

The fluorescent tennis ball remains suspended
near the edge of frame.

Then—

the ball suddenly drops.

TOK.

CUT TO IVORY.

END.

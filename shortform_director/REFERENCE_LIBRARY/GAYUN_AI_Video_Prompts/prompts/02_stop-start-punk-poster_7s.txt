Create a 7-second experimental graphic-design motion film.

STYLE:
Raw punk editorial graphic design mixed with Swiss modernist typography,
1970s–1990s public safety signage, underground music posters,
photocopied zines, screen-printed political posters and distressed street graphics.

Extremely bold oversized black grotesk sans-serif typography.
Simple universal pictograms.
Flat geometric symbols.
Hard rectangular compositions.
Aggressive cropping.
Imperfect analog printing.

Visible paper grain, faded ink, scratches, tiny registration errors,
photocopier noise, screen-print texture and slightly worn edges.

Colors are extremely limited:
dirty off-white paper,
deep black,
signal red,
emergency green.

NO glossy graphics.
NO polished 3D.
NO photorealistic environments.
NO gradients.
NO cinematic lighting.

The entire video feels like a stack of old warning posters
being violently animated to music.

MOTION LANGUAGE:

Extremely fast graphic editing.

Hard cuts.
Smash cuts.
Directional wipes created by arrows.
Typography used as transitions.
Poster layers ripping away to reveal new compositions.
Match cuts between geometric shapes.
Sudden punch-ins.
Single-frame flashes.
Mechanical typography movement.

Every transition must feel physically connected to
the graphic element from the previous shot.

NO dissolves.
NO smooth cinematic transitions.

Typography and symbols should frequently extend outside the frame.

Composition should feel intentionally uncomfortable,
bold and confrontational.

────────────────────────
AUDIO:

Original 7-second minimal industrial electronic beat.

Approximately 125–135 BPM.

Deep distorted kick.
Dry mechanical clicks.
Short analog bass impacts.
Paper ripping.
Marker scratching.
Photocopier sounds.
Metallic clacks.

No vocals.
No melody-heavy soundtrack.
No orchestral music.

Sound effects must synchronize precisely with
typography appearances, cuts, arrows and impacts.

The soundtrack should feel like experimental fashion advertising
mixed with an underground graphic-design ident.

────────────────────────

black,

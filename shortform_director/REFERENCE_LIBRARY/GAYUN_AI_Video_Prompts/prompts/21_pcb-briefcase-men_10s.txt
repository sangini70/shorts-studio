Create a 10-second surreal editorial collage animation.

CONCEPT:
Three anonymous businessmen walk endlessly across an empty cream-colored space.

Their bodies are made from vintage black-and-white newspaper photographs,
architectural engravings and coarse halftone printing.

Each businessman carries a vivid fluorescent-green briefcase
made entirely from a printed circuit board.

At first they appear to be ordinary office workers.

Gradually, the circuit boards begin connecting to their bodies.

By the end, we realize the businessmen themselves
are functioning like machines.

No photorealism.
No conventional 3D animation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ANIMATION LANGUAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This must feel like PHYSICAL PAPER COLLAGE
being animated frame by frame.

Movement should NOT be fluid Disney-style character animation.

Use:

cut-out animation,
limited walking cycles,
slightly mechanical limb articulation,
paper sliding,
photographic jump cuts,
stop-motion-like pose changes,
print registration shifts,
photocopier flashes,
hard cuts.

Individual body pieces may move like
separate pieces of cut paper.

Keep the intentional imperfections.

The halftone pattern must remain visible
during movement.

Do not smooth the characters into normal digital humans.

CAMERA & EDITING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Alternate clearly between:

No depth-of-field effects.

The composition should always feel

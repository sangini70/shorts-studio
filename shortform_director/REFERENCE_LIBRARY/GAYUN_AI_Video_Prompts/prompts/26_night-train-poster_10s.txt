Create a 10-second stylized animated metropolitan night journey.

CORE CONCEPT:

A brilliant red-orange elevated train races through a deep-blue city at night.

We begin outside the train,
then seamlessly move through a glowing window,
enter the carriage,
follow one passenger,
look through their eyes at the city,
and finally explode back outside into a spectacular wide shot.

The entire film should feel like
a living animated travel poster.

Fast.
Elegant.
Graphic.
Dreamlike.

Bold geometric editorial illustration.

Retro metropolitan advertising art.
Minimal vector-like shapes with subtle analog texture.
Stylized architectural illustration.

Extremely clean silhouettes.

NO photorealism.

Buildings are simplified geometric masses.

Very limited palette.

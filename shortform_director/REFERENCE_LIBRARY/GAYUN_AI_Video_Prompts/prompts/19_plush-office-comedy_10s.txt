Create a 10-second deadpan comedy short film featuring a REAL PHYSICAL PLUSH CREATURE working in a mundane corporate office.

CORE CONCEPT:

A strange oversized lime-green plush creature is completely exhausted at work.

It lies collapsed across its office desk,
staring lifelessly at a plastic cup of iced cola.

The creature finally drinks it.

For one glorious second,
it becomes insanely productive.

Then immediately crashes again.

The humor must be extremely dry and understated.

IMPORTANT:
The creature must always look like a REAL HANDMADE STUFFED PLUSH TOY photographed inside a REAL OFFICE.

Not CGI.
Not a cartoon.
Not smooth 3D animation.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CHARACTER DESIGN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain one consistent original plush character throughout the entire film.

Large lime-green oval plush body.

Soft fuzzy fabric.

Visible stitched seams.

Long floppy plush arms.

Short plush legs.

Small purple fabric fins along the head and back.

Large sleepy half-closed fabric eyes.

Heavy green eyelids.

Small black pupils.

Long flat purple plush mouth.

Slightly grumpy,
emotionally exhausted expression.

The character should feel physically soft,
heavy,
slightly floppy,
and imperfectly handmade.

When moving,
its fabric compresses,
wrinkles,
folds,
and drags naturally.

Do NOT make the face highly expressive.

Its permanent exhausted expression
is the joke.

0:00–0:01.4 — MONDAY

The plush creature is completely collapsed

One arm under its head.

Half-open exhausted eyes.

In front of it:

a clear plastic cup filled with dark iced cola.

Ice cubes floating inside.

Nothing happens.

Office ambience:

air conditioner hum.

keyboard typing somewhere far away.

printer noise.

PHONE RINGS somewhere.

The creature does not react.

Hold the awkward silence.

Beat.

Fabric hand slides across desk.

sssshhhh...

Beat.

Tiny plastic:

TOK.

HARD CUT.

The creature is now sitting upright.

SLUUUUURP.

Extreme close-up of its sleepy eyes.

Nothing changes.

Beat.

Then—

PUPILS SUDDENLY EXPAND.

BASS HIT.

SMASH CUT.

FAST MONTAGE.

CLACKCLACKCLACKCLACK.

Mouse moving insanely fast.

CUT.

Creature answering two office phones.

THUMP.

CUT.

CUT.

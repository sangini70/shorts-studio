10-SECOND CINEMATIC DREAMLIKE ANIMATION.
STRICT OUTPUT: 16:9 HORIZONTAL LANDSCAPE, 1920×1080.
NEVER portrait. NEVER square.

Use the reference image as the visual foundation.

STORY:
Two young friends sit together on the roof of an old dark-blue van at sunset, watching an impossibly huge full moon. At first the moment feels peaceful and nostalgic. Then they slowly realize the moon is getting closer.

VISUAL STYLE:
Preserve the dreamy illustrated atmosphere of the reference:
soft hand-painted anime-inspired editorial illustration,
subtle film grain,
deep cobalt-blue sky,
coral-pink sunset horizon,
huge pale ivory moon,
dark navy silhouettes,
soft atmospheric glow,
minimal cinematic composition,
quiet nostalgic road-trip mood.

Do NOT make it photorealistic.
Keep the SAME two characters, SAME van, SAME location and SAME clothing throughout.

0.0–3.0 sec — QUIET MOMENT

EXTREME WIDE REAR SHOT.

The old van is parked alone on an empty road.

Two friends sit shoulder-to-shoulder on the roof, seen from behind.

An enormous glowing full moon dominates the sky directly behind them.

The last orange sun is disappearing beneath the distant horizon.

Their legs gently swing over the edge of the roof.

A soft breeze moves their hair and clothes.

One character quietly leans their shoulder against the other.

CAMERA:
very slow cinematic push-in.

AUDIO:
soft evening wind,
distant crickets,
faint cooling engine ticks from the van,
gentle dreamy analog synth,
two or three sparse piano notes.

No dialogue.


3.0–5.2 sec — SOMETHING IS WRONG

HARD CUT.

SIDE MEDIUM SHOT of the two friends.

For the first time, partially reveal their faces in warm sunset light.

One friend is relaxed.

The other suddenly notices something strange.

Their smile slowly disappears.

They look upward.

The moonlight becomes noticeably brighter across their faces.

The second friend follows their gaze.

CAMERA:
slow tilt upward with their eyes.

AUDIO:
ambient music gradually fades,
wind becomes quieter,
a very subtle deep atmospheric rumble begins.


5.2–7.2 sec — THE MOON MOVES

SMASH CUT TO THEIR POV.

The gigantic moon fills most of the sky.

At first it appears still.

Then—

it visibly GROWS larger.

Not rapidly.
Slowly, impossibly, unmistakably.

The moon is approaching Earth.

Tiny stars disappear behind its expanding edge.

Its pale glow floods the landscape.

AUDIO:
deep distant celestial rumble,

mmmmmmmm—

subtle low-frequency vibration.

One friend whispers off-screen:

"...is it moving?"


7.2–8.5 sec — REALIZATION

HARD CUT.

FRONTAL CLOSE-UP OF BOTH FACES.

Their faces are now illuminated almost entirely by cold white moonlight.

They stare upward.

Eyes widening.

Neither moves for half a second.

Then they slowly look at each other.

Complete silence.

One quietly says:

"Yeah."

Beat.


8.5–10.0 sec — TOO CLOSE

SMASH CUT TO EXTREME WIDE REAR SHOT.

Return to almost the SAME composition as the opening.

But now—

the moon has become ENORMOUS.

Its curved surface extends beyond the top and sides of the entire 16:9 frame.

The van and the two friends are tiny silhouettes against it.

The landscape is completely washed in pale moonlight.

A powerful wind suddenly rushes toward them.

Their hair and clothes violently flutter.

The two friends instinctively grab onto each other.

One shouts:

"GO! GO! GO!"

They scramble toward the edge of the van roof—

CUT TO BLACK before they can climb down.

AUDIO:

WHOOMMMMM—

powerful celestial bass,
rushing wind,
metal suspension creak from the van,

"GO! GO! GO!"

CUT TO BLACK.

Then one final tiny sound:

car keys jingling.

END.

"GO! GO! GO!"

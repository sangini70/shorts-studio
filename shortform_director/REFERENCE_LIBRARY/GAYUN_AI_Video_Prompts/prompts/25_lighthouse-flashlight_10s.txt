Create a 10-second surreal retro-futurist animated short film.

CORE CONCEPT:

In the middle of a vast midnight ocean stands a lighthouse.

But the lighthouse does not have a normal lantern room.

Its upper tower transforms seamlessly into a gigantic pale-blue human hand,
holding a real flashlight horizontally.

The lighthouse IS the arm.

The flashlight beam slowly searches across the night ocean.

At first, nothing happens.

Then the beam discovers something impossible beneath the water.

The lighthouse turns the flashlight downward.

The ocean becomes transparent wherever the light touches,
revealing an enormous hidden underwater city.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL STYLE — CRITICAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the visual language of the reference.

Dreamlike retro-futurist editorial illustration.
1970s–1980s conceptual poster art.
Vintage science-fiction magazine cover.
Airbrushed graphic illustration.
Surreal advertising artwork.

NOT photorealistic.

Simplified geometric forms.
Softly modeled shapes.
Clean silhouettes.
Minimal environmental detail.

Subtle analog paper grain across every frame.
Fine printed noise.
Slightly faded pigments.
Soft airbrush gradients.
Very subtle halftone texture.

Extremely controlled palette:

midnight navy,
deep ultramarine,
electric cobalt,
powder blue,
lavender,
pale violet,
warm coral-red,
soft cream-white.

The flashlight is the brightest object.

Its light is:
warm white at the source,
pale lavender at the edges,
slightly pink where it touches fog.

The atmosphere should feel:
quiet,
lonely,
mysterious,
dreamlike,
slightly uncanny.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:00–0:01.5 — THE LIGHTHOUSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME WIDE SHOT.

A vast dark-blue ocean at night.

Tiny geometric islands in the distance.

Sparse stylized clouds.

A few tiny stars.

In the center stands a striped lighthouse.

White and muted coral-red bands.

Camera slowly pushes toward it.

A tiny boat floats in the foreground.

One small silhouetted person stands inside the boat,
looking toward the lighthouse.

Then we notice something strange:

the top of the lighthouse is not architecture.

It becomes a GIANT HUMAN HAND.

The pale-blue hand holds a flashlight.

CLICK.

The flashlight turns on.

A powerful cone of pale lavender-white light
shoots across the frame.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:01.5–0:02.8 — SEARCHING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WIDE SIDE SHOT.

The enormous hand slowly rotates.

The flashlight beam sweeps across the ocean.

The beam behaves like a giant searchlight.

As it passes:

clouds become illuminated,
mountains briefly appear,
water glows violet,
tiny distant islands emerge from darkness.

The camera follows the moving beam.

Soft mechanical flashlight movement:

rrrrrrr—

Ocean ambience underneath.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:02.8–0:03.8 — SOMETHING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The flashlight suddenly stops.

Complete stillness.

The beam points toward an apparently empty section of ocean.

The person in the boat looks toward it.

A strange low tone appears.

mmmmmmmm—

Under the illuminated water:

a tiny geometric shape becomes visible.

Then another.

Then another.

The hand slightly tilts the flashlight downward.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:03.8–0:05.2 — UNDER THE WATER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MATCH CUT.

Camera dives beneath the ocean surface.

WHOOSH.

We remain INSIDE the flashlight beam.

Outside the beam:

almost complete ultramarine darkness.

Inside the beam:

an enormous underwater landscape slowly appears.

Geometric towers.

Domes.

Bridges.

Impossible staircases.

A completely submerged retro-futurist city.

Everything rendered in:

powder blue,
lavender,
pale cyan.

The buildings look like
forgotten architectural illustrations.

Tiny bubbles float upward.

No fish.

No people.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:05.2–0:06.3 — THE WINDOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Camera travels deeper through the illuminated city.

The flashlight beam passes across
hundreds of dark windows.

All empty.

Then—

ONE WINDOW IS LIT.

Warm coral-orange.

Camera rapidly pushes toward it.

Inside:

a tiny silhouetted human figure.

Standing perfectly still.

Looking upward.

Directly toward the flashlight.

Beat.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:06.3–0:07.2 — RESPONSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME CLOSE-UP.

The underwater figure raises one arm.

In their hand:

a tiny flashlight.

CLICK.

A narrow orange beam shoots upward.

CUT TO SURFACE.

The person in the boat sees
a tiny orange point of light
coming from beneath the ocean.

They freeze.

The giant lighthouse hand freezes too.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:07.2–0:08.3 — TWO LIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME WIDE SHOT.

Beautiful symmetrical composition.

Above water:

the enormous lighthouse flashlight
projects a pale violet beam downward.

Below water:

the tiny orange flashlight
projects a warm beam upward.

The two beams meet
exactly at the ocean surface.

For one second,
they form a perfect glowing line.

Music swells.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:08.3–0:09.2 — BLACKOUT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Suddenly—

CLICK.

The underwater orange light disappears.

Then—

CLICK.

The lighthouse flashlight shuts off.

Complete darkness.

Only stars remain.

Silence.

Hold for half a second.

Then:

CLICK.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:09.2–0:10.0 — FINAL REVEAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The lighthouse flashlight turns on again.

But now it is pointing DIRECTLY AT CAMERA.

Huge white-lavender light fills the frame.

Camera rapidly pulls backward.

We discover something impossible:

the entire ocean,
lighthouse,
boat,
mountains and underwater city

are contained INSIDE
ANOTHER gigantic flashlight beam.

Far outside the world,
a second enormous silhouetted hand
is holding another flashlight.

Our entire world was simply
something being searched for.

The outer flashlight begins moving away.

The tiny world disappears into darkness.

CLICK.

BLACK.

END.

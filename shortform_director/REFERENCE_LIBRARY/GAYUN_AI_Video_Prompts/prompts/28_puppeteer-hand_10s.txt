0:00–0:03.0 — I AM IN CONTROL

Extreme wide editorial composition.

A tiny office worker stands on a gigantic circular computer key beneath an enormous robotic hand.

He types two commands into his laptop.

TIK. TIK.

The giant robotic hand responds precisely.

First command:
one robotic finger bends.

KRRR.

Second command:
the robotic wrist rotates.

KRRRK.

The worker looks upward.

A subtle confident expression.

He believes the machine is obeying him.

Low analog electronic pulse underneath.

0:03.0–0:05.0 — SOMETHING IS WRONG

Medium side shot.

The worker stops typing.

He slowly begins closing the laptop.

CLICK—

Before it closes completely:

the gigantic robotic finger moves by itself.

KRRRRRRR...

The worker freezes.

Do not cut immediately.

Hold the uncomfortable silence.

He slowly raises his eyes toward the machine.

The music completely disappears.

Only a faint electrical hum remains.

0:05.0–0:07.0 — THE DISCOVERY

Slow camera movement behind the worker.

For the first time,
reveal ONE extremely thin dark cable attached to the back of his shirt.

Follow the cable upward.

It disappears into the enormous robotic hand.

The worker slowly looks over his shoulder.

He notices it.

His expression changes from confidence to confusion.

Then—

the cable becomes tight.

TNNNNNG—

Hold for a fraction of a second.

0:07.0–0:09.0 — REVERSAL

The robotic index finger slowly moves downward.

KRRRRRR...

At exactly the same time,

the cable pulls the worker's arm upward.

He tries to resist.

His arm continues moving.

TINY HUMAN FINGER DESCENDING.

ROBOT.

HUMAN.

ROBOT.

HUMAN.

0:09.0–0:10.0 — FINAL REVEAL

Instant extreme wide shot.

For the first time,

The enormous robotic hand hangs above like a puppeteer's hand.

Several thin cables descend from its fingers.

One attached to the worker's wrist.

One to his shoulder.

One to his back.

The tiny worker stands below,
still holding the laptop,
his body positioned like a marionette.

The giant computer key beneath him slowly depresses.

CHK—

The worker descends with it.

CLICK.

Everything stops.

Hold the final graphic image.

Complete silence.

CUT TO BLACK.

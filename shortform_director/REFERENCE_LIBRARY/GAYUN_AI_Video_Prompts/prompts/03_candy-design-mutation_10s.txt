Create a 10-second high-energy experimental candy fashion commercial.

SUBJECT:
A carefully arranged flat-lay composition of translucent heart-shaped
red lollipops scattered across a clean studio surface.

Several lollipops are unwrapped.
Several remain inside clear crinkled plastic wrappers.
White sticks create strong diagonal lines through the composition.

The candy arrangement remains essentially THE SAME throughout the film.

IMPORTANT:
Do not continuously animate or rearrange the candies.

Instead, create excitement through extremely rapid changes in:
COLOR,
TYPOGRAPHY,
PRINT TEXTURE,
CROPPING,
GRAPHIC OVERLAYS,
EXPOSURE,
and EDITORIAL TREATMENT.

It should feel like one photograph being repeatedly redesigned
into dozens of different magazine covers and advertising posters.

Premium experimental fashion advertising.
Contemporary candy campaign.

Pop-art energy.
Swiss typography.
90s magazine advertising.

Risograph printing.
Photocopied punk graphics.
Halftone magazine printing.
Overexposed flash photography.

Glossy translucent candy remains tactile and photographic.

Hard direct studio flash.
Sharp cast shadows.
Beautiful specular highlights through transparent red candy.
Slightly imperfect analog print texture.

The underlying composition remains recognizable throughout.

EDITING LANGUAGE

Extremely fast editorial cuts.

2-frame flashes.
3-frame flashes.
Freeze frames.
Jump cuts.
Hard cuts.
Flash-frame transitions.
Abrupt crop changes.
Instant color replacement.
Graphic posterization.
Typography replacement.
Print-process transformations.

Occasionally zoom into EXACTLY the same photograph,
then instantly return to the wide composition.

Do NOT create random camera movement.

The excitement comes from DESIGN MUTATION,
not physical camera movement.

During the fastest section,
the viewer should feel as if dozens of graphic designers
are redesigning the same advertisement every second.

────────────────────────────
TYPOGRAPHY

Typography is a PRIMARY visual subject.

Use dramatic contrast between type styles.

Alternate rapidly between:

bold grotesk,
Helvetica-like Swiss sans,
ultra-condensed sans,
extended geometric sans,
high-fashion Didone serif,
italic serif,
monospace,
typewriter,
rough marker lettering,
photocopied punk lettering,
stamped typography.

Typography must feel intentionally art-directed.

Allow letters to extend beyond frame boundaries.

Use extreme scale differences.

Do not keep typography centered all the time.

Typography may interact with candy:
behind it,
in front of it,
cropped around it,
or partially hidden by it.

CRITICAL CONSISTENCY RULE

The heart-shaped lollipops,
their positions,
white sticks,
clear wrappers,
and overall composition
must remain visually consistent.

Treat the product photograph as a FIXED MASTER IMAGE.

Transform the GRAPHIC DESIGN around and over it.

Do not regenerate a different arrangement for every cut.

The audience should subconsciously recognize:
THIS IS THE SAME IMAGE
being continuously reinterpreted.

STRICTLY AVOID:

random product movement,
melting candy,
changing candy shapes,
extra lollipops appearing,
missing lollipops,
warped sticks,
warped wrappers,
AI morphing,
slow dreamy transitions,
cross dissolves,
cinematic landscapes,
realistic people,
hands,
3D typography,
generic corporate graphics,
constant camera movement.

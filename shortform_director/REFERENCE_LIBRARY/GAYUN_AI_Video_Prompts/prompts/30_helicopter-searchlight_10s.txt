10-SECOND CINEMATIC GRAPHIC NOIR ANIMATION.
STRICT OUTPUT: 16:9 HORIZONTAL LANDSCAPE, 1920×1080.
AUDIO INCLUDED.
CLEAR SCENE TRANSITIONS ARE MANDATORY.

Use the reference image as the main visual language.

STYLE:
minimalist editorial noir illustration,
deep black night,
monochrome cyan / teal searchlights,
extreme graphic contrast,
screen-printed poster texture,
subtle paper grain,
sharp geometric shadows,
1960s–1980s thriller poster aesthetic,
mysterious surveillance atmosphere.

Keep ONE SAME adult fugitive throughout.
Mostly represented as a small black silhouette.
Do not introduce additional people.

STORY:
A lone person runs through complete darkness.
A distant searchlight begins hunting for them.
They try to hide.
Then the source of the light is revealed:
a helicopter.
The beam finds them.


0.0–2.3 SEC — RUN

BLACK SCREEN.

We hear footsteps BEFORE we see anything.

tap—tap—tap—tap—

HARD CUT.

EXTREME WIDE SIDE SHOT.

A tiny black human silhouette runs across a narrow dark ridge.

Almost the entire frame is black.

Only a faint cyan edge separates the ground from the sky.

The runner repeatedly looks behind them.

CAMERA:
slow lateral tracking.

AUDIO:
rapid footsteps,
strained breathing,
distant cold wind,
very subtle low-frequency pulse.

No music yet.


2.3–4.2 SEC — THE LIGHT

SMASH CUT.

CLOSE-UP OF THE RUNNER'S FACE.

For the first and only clear facial glimpse:

sweat,
wide eyes,
heavy breathing.

Suddenly—

a thin cyan beam sweeps across the background behind their face.

Their eyes follow it.

They freeze.

AUDIO:

WHOOOOSH—

distant rotor noise slowly becomes audible.

The runner whispers:

"No..."


4.2–6.2 SEC — HIDE

HARD CUT TO HIGH-ANGLE WIDE SHOT.

The runner dives behind a jagged black rock formation.

A gigantic triangular searchlight sweeps across the landscape.

LEFT...

CENTER...

RIGHT...

The cyan beam narrowly misses them.

The runner presses against the rock.

Complete stillness.

CAMERA:
static overhead composition.

AUDIO:
helicopter rotor growing louder,

THUP—THUP—THUP—THUP—

wind,
one nervous breath.

The searchlight passes.

Silence for half a second.


6.2–7.6 SEC — FALSE SAFETY

HARD CUT.

MEDIUM SHOT.

The runner slowly emerges from behind the rock.

They look upward.

Nothing.

Darkness.

They exhale in relief.

Then—

a tiny cyan point appears directly on their chest.

They look down at it.

Their expression changes instantly.

AUDIO:
one tiny electronic tracking tone:

BEEP.

Music begins with one deep bass hit.


7.6–10.0 SEC — FOUND

SMASH CUT TO EXTREME WIDE LOW-ANGLE SHOT.

REVEAL THE HELICOPTER.

A black helicopter silhouette hangs high in the night sky.

Its rotors violently cut through the darkness.

A MASSIVE CYAN SEARCHLIGHT suddenly blasts downward.

WHOOM!

The runner is caught perfectly in the center.

They attempt to run—

but the searchlight follows them precisely.

Their enormous distorted shadow stretches across the ground.

CAMERA RAPIDLY PULLS BACK.

The entire composition gradually becomes increasingly graphic and poster-like:

tiny hunted silhouette below,
huge triangular cyan beam,
helicopter above,
almost pure black negative space.

During the final 0.5 seconds,
large condensed typography becomes visible INSIDE the beam:

"FOUND"

The runner stops.

Looks upward.

CUT TO BLACK.


AUDIO:
THUP THUP THUP THUP helicopter rotor,
powerful rushing air,
searchlight electrical ignition,
footsteps,
heavy breathing,
deep cinematic bass,
subtle analog thriller synth.

At the exact final frame:

all sound abruptly stops.

BLACK.


EDITING STRUCTURE:

RUNNING SILHOUETTE
→ SMASH CUT
→ FACE CLOSE-UP + LIGHT PASSES
→ HARD CUT
→ OVERHEAD HIDING SHOT
→ HARD CUT
→ FALSE SAFETY
→ BEEP
→ SMASH CUT
→ HELICOPTER REVEAL
→ SEARCHLIGHT CATCHES HIM
→ GRAPHIC POSTER COMPOSITION
→ BLACK.

IMPORTANT:
Actual cuts between shots are mandatory.
Do NOT generate one continuous camera movement.
Do NOT reveal the helicopter immediately.
The helicopter must remain mysterious until approximately 7.6 seconds.
The searchlight itself should feel like the antagonist during the first half.

Keep the visual information extremely minimal.
Large areas of pure black negative space.
Only black, dark navy and luminous cyan.
No unnecessary buildings.
No crowds.
No vehicles except the helicopter.
No weapons.
No explosions.

The final shot should strongly resemble a sophisticated animated editorial thriller poster.

DIALOGUE:
Only one whispered word:
"No..."

TYPOGRAPHY:
Only final word:
"FOUND"

NO OTHER TEXT.
NO LOGOS.
NO SUBTITLES.
NO WATERMARK.

STRICTLY 10 SECONDS.
STRICTLY 16:9 HORIZONTAL.

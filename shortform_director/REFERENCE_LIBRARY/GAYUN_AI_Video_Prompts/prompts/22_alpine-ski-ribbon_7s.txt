Create a 7-second dreamlike alpine ski animation with multiple cinematic shots.

CORE IDEA:

A lone skier descends through an impossible minimalist mountain landscape.

The snow does not behave like an ordinary ski slope.

It forms enormous smooth white ribbons floating through a vast cobalt-blue world.

As the skier carves through the landscape,
his trail gradually becomes part of the giant flowing white composition.

The final reveal shows that his entire route has created
one enormous elegant S-shaped ribbon across the blue mountain.

No dialogue.
No typography.
No logos.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL STYLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the exact visual language of the reference:

dreamlike minimalist alpine editorial illustration,
soft vintage ski-poster aesthetic,
delicate pointillist airbrush texture,
fine stochastic grain,
millions of tiny white stippled particles,
powdery printed-paper surface,
subtle screen-print feeling.

Dominant color:
deep saturated cobalt blue.

Snow:
luminous ivory white,
soft cream-white highlights,
very pale icy blue shadows.

Skier:
small relative to the environment.

White helmet.
Warm ivory ski jacket.
Muted yellow trousers fading toward warm coral-red near the boots.
Long coral-red skis.
Thin red ski poles.

Extremely limited palette.

Large uninterrupted areas of cobalt-blue negative space.

The snow should appear almost luminous against the blue.

NO photorealistic mountain photography.
NO realistic rocky mountains.
NO trees.
NO ski resort.
NO buildings.

The world is abstract,
minimal,
surreal,
clean,
enormous.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:00–0:01.3 — THE CARVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME WIDE TOP-DOWN SHOT.

The skier is tiny inside an enormous cobalt-blue landscape.

A narrow white snow ribbon curves diagonally through the frame.

The skier rapidly carves left.

SHHHHHHH—

A beautiful plume of white powder explodes behind him,
made from thousands of tiny stippled particles.

He shifts his weight.

Carves sharply right.

The skis cut two delicate parallel lines through the snow.

Camera gently tracks above him.

Minimal music begins:

one warm analog synth chord
and a soft rhythmic pulse.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:01.3–0:02.2 — SPEED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HARD CUT.

EXTREME LOW SIDE ANGLE,
very close to the snow.

The skier blasts across frame.

His coral skis dominate the foreground.

Fine snow particles spray directly toward camera.

His knees compress.

Poles sweep backward.

WHOOSH.

The background is almost entirely cobalt blue.

For approximately 0.2 seconds,
the skier disappears behind his own powder cloud.

CUT THROUGH THE WHITE CLOUD.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:02.2–0:03.1 — THE DROP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The white powder becomes
ANOTHER WHITE LANDSCAPE.

MATCH CUT.

WIDE FRONTAL SHOT.

The skier suddenly approaches the edge
of a smooth sculptural snow ridge.

Beyond it:

nothing but enormous blue negative space.

He reaches the edge.

His body lowers.

Skis bend slightly.

Then—

he launches.

Music briefly disappears.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:03.1–0:04.0 — AIR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME WIDE SHOT.

The skier floats through a gigantic cobalt-blue void.

He is extremely small.

Almost graphic.

No visible mountain underneath him.

Only a distant curved white snow ribbon
far below.

Tiny snow particles continue floating around him.

Complete quiet except for soft wind.

His body rotates very slightly.

Skis remain parallel.

One tiny analog synth note rises.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:04.0–0:05.0 — LANDING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CUT TO OVERHEAD SHOT.

The white ribbon rushes upward toward him.

He lands perfectly.

FWOOMP.

A massive soft powder explosion.

The white plume temporarily fills the entire frame.

The beat returns.

He immediately enters another deep carving turn.

LEFT.

RIGHT.

His red poles create tiny graphic accents
against the white snow.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:05.0–0:06.0 — IMPOSSIBLE SLOPE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Camera rapidly pulls upward.

The skier becomes smaller.

And smaller.

And smaller.

As the camera rises,
we discover that the snowfield is not a conventional mountain.

It is an enormous flowing WHITE RIBBON
winding through an infinite cobalt-blue surface.

The skier continues moving along it.

His powder trail extends behind him.

The camera keeps pulling back.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:06.0–0:07.0 — FINAL REVEAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME AERIAL WIDE SHOT.

The skier is now only a tiny colored dot.

The complete route is revealed.

A gigantic elegant white S-shaped trail
flows across the entire cobalt-blue composition.

The skier completes one final tiny turn
at the bottom of the S.

A small powder cloud:

puff.

The music resolves with one soft warm chord.

Wind fades.

Hold the final image for a moment.

It should look like a finished
mid-century alpine travel poster.

CUT TO COBALT BLUE.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
MOTION LANGUAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Skiing movement must feel elegant and physically convincing:

deep carving,
controlled edge changes,
natural knee compression,
balanced upper body,
correct pole positioning,
parallel skis,
powder reacting to turns.

However, the ENVIRONMENT remains surreal and graphic.

Contrast:

PHYSICALLY BELIEVABLE SKIER
+
IMPOSSIBLE ABSTRACT LANDSCAPE.

The skier should never look like a cartoon character.

Movement is smooth and graceful,
not frantic.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CAMERA LANGUAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use clearly different cinematic compositions:

extreme aerial,
snow-level tracking,
frontal wide,
extreme negative-space jump shot,
overhead landing,
massive final pull-back.

Use hard cuts and one powder-cloud match transition.

No generic dissolves.

The scale must constantly alternate:

tiny skier in enormous environment

→

extreme close action

→

tiny skier again.

This contrast is essential.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TEXTURE CONSISTENCY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CRITICAL:

Maintain the delicate printed stipple texture
throughout EVERY shot.

Cobalt areas contain subtle fine grain.

Snow edges dissolve into thousands
of microscopic white dots.

Powder clouds are not realistic volumetric CGI.

They are constructed from
fine white pointillist particles,
airbrushed stippling,
soft printed grain.

Even during close action,
the image must preserve the same
editorial illustration aesthetic.

Do not become photorealistic during close-ups.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AUDIO

Minimal elegant electronic soundtrack.

Warm analog synthesizer.
Very soft electronic pulse.
Sparse low-frequency beat.
Airy high-frequency texture.

No vocals.
No epic orchestral music.

Sound design is important:

ski edges cutting snow:
SHHHHH—

powder spray:
soft granular rush.

close fly-by:
WHOOSH.

landing:
deep soft FWOOMP.

final turn:
gentle snow spray.

while the skier is suspended in the blue void.

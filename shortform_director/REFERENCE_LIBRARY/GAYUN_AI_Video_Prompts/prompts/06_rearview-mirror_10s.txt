Create a 10-second cinematic 2D animated short film with a clear story,
multiple shots, visible character faces, spoken dialogue, ambient sound,
and seamless visual continuity.

NO typography.
NO captions.
NO titles.
NO written text anywhere.

STORY:
A young woman is driving alone across an empty desert highway.
She casually adjusts her rear-view mirror.

For one brief moment, she sees another woman sitting silently
in the back seat.

She turns around.

Nobody is there.

She looks back into the mirror.

The mysterious woman is now much closer.

────────────────────────────
VISUAL STYLE

Stylized minimalist 2D editorial animation.

Strong flat vector illustration aesthetic.

Characters, vehicles, landscapes and faces are constructed from
large simplified geometric shapes and clean silhouettes.

Very limited color palette:
deep slate gray,
charcoal black,
electric cobalt blue,
pale sky blue,
burnt orange,
rust brown,
small warm cream highlights.

Skin is rendered primarily in burnt orange and deep rust shadows.

Faces are simplified but expressive:
clean facial silhouettes,
minimal nose,
small mouth,
simple graphic eyes,
limited facial details.

No realistic skin texture.
No pores.
No detailed hair strands.

Hair is represented as large solid graphic shapes.

Hard-edged shadows.
Almost no texture.
Minimal gradients.
Clean graphic color blocking.

The entire film must look like the SAME illustrator
animated every frame.

NOT photorealistic.
NOT 3D CGI.
NOT anime.
NOT children's cartoon.

Sophisticated contemporary editorial animation.

0:04–0:05.5 — SOMETHING IS WRONG

POV OF THE REAR-VIEW MIRROR.

The highway is visible behind the car.

For half a second everything looks normal.

Then—

A WOMAN is visible sitting perfectly still

Only her upper body and face are visible.

No smile.

No movement.

CUT TO:

EXTREME CLOSE-UP OF DRIVER'S FACE.

Her eyes widen.

DRIVER:

────────────────────────────
0:07–0:09 — THE MIRROR

CLOSE-UP.

Driver's face from the front.

Her expression is unsettled.

She hesitates.

Her eyes slowly move upward

CUT TO:

REAR-VIEW MIRROR POV.

Her face occupies almost the entire mirror.

Completely motionless.

The mysterious woman quietly whispers:

"Almost."

Her voice is extremely close,
as if whispered directly beside the driver's ear.

0:09–0:10 — END

SMASH CUT TO DRIVER.

Extreme close-up.

Her eyes widen.

She rapidly turns—

CUT TO BLACK
before we see what happens.

SOUND:

TIRES SCREECH.

A sharp inhale.

Music delivers one deep distorted bass note.

BOOM.

Immediate silence.

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY

DRIVER:
young adult woman,
burnt-orange graphic skin tone,
dark nearly-black geometric shoulder-length hair,
simple cobalt-blue shirt,
minimal facial features,
small dark eyes,
graphic nose and mouth.

Her appearance MUST remain identical across every shot.

MYSTERIOUS WOMAN:
young adult female silhouette,
very dark charcoal clothing,
rust-orange simplified face,
long black geometric hair,
emotionless expression,
minimal dark eyes.

She must remain visually consistent between both mirror appearances.

Do NOT change either character's face,
hair,
clothing,
age,
or proportions between shots.

────────────────────────────
CINEMATOGRAPHY

Use intentional cinematic coverage:

wide establishing shot,
medium character shot,
profile shot,
extreme close-up,
driver POV,
rear-view mirror POV,
reaction shot.

Use HARD CUTS between shots.

Camera movement should remain restrained.

Occasional subtle push-in during suspense.

No random camera motion.
No excessive zooming.
No morphing between shots.

Maintain correct spatial continuity inside the vehicle.

The rear-view mirror must consistently show
the physical back seat and rear window.

────────────────────────────
ANIMATION

Premium limited 2D animation.

Approximately 12–18 visually meaningful poses per second,
with intentionally smooth interpolation where appropriate.

Natural subtle character movement:
blinking,
breathing,
finger movement,
hair movement,
eye movement,
small head movements.

During suspense,
reduce movement dramatically.

The mysterious woman should be unnaturally still.

Her stillness should contrast with the subtle vibration
of the moving car.

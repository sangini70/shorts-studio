7-SECOND VERTICAL CINEMATIC COMEDY VIDEO, 9:16.

Create a short photorealistic absurd comedy commercial featuring the SAME tiny golden hamster throughout the entire video.

VISUAL STYLE:
Photorealistic miniature-world photography, realistic soft golden hamster fur, tiny pink paws, slightly oversized cute body proportions. The hamster wears a tiny beige baseball cap, silver over-ear headphones, and a white weightlifting belt. Real modern commercial gym environment, black rubber flooring, squat racks and weight machines in the background. Strong contrast between the extremely tiny cute hamster and ridiculously heavy professional gym equipment. Natural indoor gym lighting, shallow depth of field, realistic shadows, slightly cinematic handheld camera. Keep the hamster's appearance, clothing, proportions, barbell and environment completely consistent between shots.

STORY:

0.0–2.0 sec — PREPARATION
Extreme low-angle close-up at floor level.
The hamster stands underneath an enormous loaded barbell, looking ridiculously small compared with the giant black weight plates.
It grips the bar with both tiny paws.
Its cheeks puff slightly as it prepares.
Slow cinematic push-in toward its determined face.
The hamster adjusts its stance and takes one tiny breath.

AUDIO:
quiet gym ambience, distant weights clanking,
tiny squeak,
subtle tension-building bass pulse:
"dum… dum…"

HARD CUT to a dramatic side angle.

The hamster suddenly performs an absurdly powerful clean-and-press.
The giant barbell rises from the floor.
Its tiny legs shake slightly under the impossible weight.
The huge black plates wobble realistically.

Camera quickly tracks upward with the barbell.

At 4 seconds the hamster successfully locks the gigantic barbell overhead.

AUDIO:
fast metallic barbell movement,
heavy plates rattling,
hamster makes a tiny strained:
"NNGH!"

Then a satisfying heavy metallic:
"CLANK."

Music suddenly kicks into a ridiculously heroic miniature sports anthem — punchy drums, short brass hit and energetic bass.

4.8–7.0 sec — COMEDIC PAYOFF
SMASH CUT to an extreme close-up of the hamster's face.

It is still holding the enormous barbell overhead.

For one second it looks intensely serious.

Then the hamster slowly turns its eyes toward the camera.

Tiny pause.

It gives one extremely subtle confident nod.

CUT to a wider low-angle hero shot:
the tiny hamster proudly standing underneath the gigantic barbell like a professional Olympic champion.

The huge weights wobble slightly above its head.

AUDIO:
heroic music reaches a tiny triumphant peak.

A distant confused human voice from somewhere in the gym says:
"…bro?"

The hamster makes one tiny satisfied:
"squeak."

CUT TO BLACK exactly on the squeak.

EDITING:
Three clearly separated shots.
Hard cinematic cuts, no morphing between camera angles.
Comedic timing created by the contrast between dramatic sports filmmaking and the tiny hamster.
First shot builds anticipation, second shot contains the action, third shot delivers the joke.
Fast but readable pacing.
No unnecessary events.

Create a 10-second surreal retro editorial animation about escaping a human sorting system.

CORE STORY:

Identical red human figures lie motionless inside capsule-shaped slots
moving along an enormous industrial platform.

They are being transported like products.

Every body is identical.

Every capsule moves in perfect synchronization.

Then one person opens their eyes.

They realize what is happening.

They break out of the capsule,
run across the moving system,
and jump beyond its edge.

For the first time,
one human leaves the pattern.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL STYLE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the exact visual language of the reference image.

Mid-century editorial illustration.
1960s–1970s conceptual magazine art.
Retro-futurist corporate illustration.
Screen-printed graphic design.
Vintage scientific diagram aesthetic.
Analog collage.
Coarse halftone printing.

Extremely limited palette:

muted seafoam green,
dusty turquoise,
pale mint,
deep charcoal black,
bright vermilion red,
small amounts of dirty white.

Human figures are simplified RED SILHOUETTES.

No facial detail except when absolutely necessary for storytelling.

Bodies are graphic,
flat,
elongated,
minimal,
almost like pictograms.

Capsules are rounded rectangular white containers
with black interiors and pale mint highlights.

The enormous platform is covered in
dense black halftone dots.

Visible paper grain.
Screen-print texture.
Slight ink misregistration.
Rough printed edges.
Subtle faded pigment.

NO photorealism.
NO glossy 3D.
NO modern sci-fi.

Every frame should resemble
an animated vintage editorial poster.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:00–0:01.5 — THE SYSTEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME WIDE ISOMETRIC SHOT.

An enormous diagonal platform extends beyond the frame.

Rows of capsule-shaped slots.

Inside each capsule:

an identical red human figure
lying perfectly still.

The entire platform slowly moves forward
like a giant conveyor belt.

Mechanical rhythm:

KUNK.

KUNK.

KUNK.

With every beat,
all capsules advance exactly one position.

Camera slowly tracks diagonally across the system.

Hundreds more capsules become visible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:01.5–0:02.6 — IDENTICAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HARD CUT.

TOP-DOWN CLOSE-UP.

Three capsules pass beneath camera.

First person:

motionless.

KUNK.

Second person:

motionless.

KUNK.

Third person:

motionless.

KUNK.

Then a fourth capsule enters.

Same red body.

But—

one hand moves slightly.

Tiny finger twitch.

The conveyor continues.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:02.6–0:03.6 — AWAKE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EXTREME CLOSE-UP.

Minimal red human face in profile.

Almost no facial detail.

One simple black eye.

The eye suddenly opens.

CLICK.

Complete silence for 0.2 seconds.

The person looks sideways.

Through the transparent capsule wall
they see another identical body.

Then another.

Then another.

Camera rapidly pulls backward.

Dozens.

Hundreds.

All identical.

Mechanical rhythm returns.

KUNK.

KUNK.

KUNK.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:03.6–0:04.7 — BREAK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The awakened person suddenly pushes
against the capsule cover.

Nothing.

Push again.

THUNK.

A thin crack appears.

One more violent push.

CRACK!

The white capsule lid flies upward
like a flat piece of printed paper.

The red figure sits up.

Every other human remains perfectly motionless.

An alarm begins:

BEEP.

BEEP.

BEEP.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:04.7–0:05.8 — RUN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SIDE VIEW.

The red figure jumps from the capsule.

LANDS.

Immediately runs across the moving platform.

Graphic cut-out running cycle.

No realistic anatomy.

Behind them,
capsules continue moving mechanically.

Camera tracks sideways.

The runner leaps over:

one capsule.

two capsules.

three capsules.

Each contains another identical version of them.

The mechanical soundtrack accelerates.

KUNK-KUNK-KUNK-KUNK.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:05.8–0:06.8 — SYSTEM REACTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OVERHEAD SHOT.

Suddenly the conveyor system recognizes the escape.

Several empty capsule lids rotate toward the runner.

Their black interiors point toward them
like abstract surveillance eyes.

BEEP.

All capsule rows suddenly change direction.

CLACK!

The geometric platform begins rearranging itself.

Sections slide horizontally.

Paths close.

New walls appear.

The runner changes direction sharply.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:06.8–0:07.8 — DEAD END
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WIDE SHOT.

The runner reaches the outer black edge
of the enormous platform.

STOP.

Beyond it:

an endless empty seafoam-green void.

No floor.

No architecture.

Nothing.

Behind the runner,
the conveyor system approaches.

Rows of identical capsules move closer.

KUNK.

KUNK.

KUNK.

The figure looks back.

Then looks into the empty void.

A distorted mechanical voice says:

"RETURN."

The figure remains still.

Beat.

Then quietly:

"No."

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:07.8–0:08.7 — JUMP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The figure runs.

Three steps.

STEP.

STEP.

STEP.

They jump from the platform.

WHOOSH.

EXTREME WIDE SHOT.

Tiny red human silhouette
floating through an enormous mint-green field.

The black platform rapidly disappears behind them.

All mechanical sound instantly stops.

Only soft air.

For the first time in the film,
the body is no longer rigid.

Arms extend.

Legs loosen.

The figure becomes expressive and free.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
0:08.7–0:10.0 — OUTSIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MATCH CUT.

The falling red silhouette becomes
a WALKING red silhouette.

There is suddenly ground beneath their feet.

Minimal pale mint surface.

They calmly walk toward the right.

Huge empty negative space.

Camera remains static.

Behind them,
far in the distance,
the enormous black conveyor platform
continues transporting hundreds of identical bodies.

KUNK.

KUNK.

KUNK.

But very quietly now.

The escaped person continues walking.

Then—

one capsule in the distant system opens.

A SECOND tiny red person sits up.

The first person stops.

Looks back.

CUT TO SEAFOAM GREEN.

END.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ANIMATION LANGUAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This must NOT look like conventional character animation.

Use:

limited-frame cutout animation,
screen-printed silhouettes,
mechanical pose changes,
paper-collage movement,
graphic sliding transformations,
isometric geometric motion.

Humans should remain simplified graphic forms.

Running should use approximately
4–6 distinct illustrated poses,
creating a slightly rough editorial animation feeling.

Capsules move in rigid mechanical increments.

The contrast is important:

SYSTEM = rigid, synchronized, geometric.

ESCAPED HUMAN = increasingly organic, irregular, expressive.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CAMERA & EDITING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use distinct compositions:

extreme isometric establishing shot,
top-down capsule sequence,
facial macro,
rapid pull-back,
side-tracking escape,
overhead system reaction,
wide edge shot,
extreme negative-space jump,
minimal final composition.

Mostly HARD CUTS.

Use one MATCH CUT during the jump.

No smooth cinematic orbiting.

No handheld camera.

No depth of field.

Keep the graphic poster composition readable.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AUDIO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Begin with NO conventional music.

Construct rhythm from industrial sounds:

mechanical conveyor impacts,
relay clicks,
plastic capsule locks,
electrical buzz,
alarm beeps.

KUNK — KUNK — KUNK.

Gradually these sounds become
a minimalist experimental electronic beat.

During escape:
tempo increases.

At the edge:
music disappears.

Mechanical voice:

"RETURN."

Human voice:

"No."

The word "No" should be calm,
quiet and completely undramatic.

When the figure jumps:

complete removal of mechanical sound.

Only gentle wind.

During the final shot,
introduce one extremely soft warm analog synth chord.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CRITICAL COLOR RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use ONLY:

SEAFOAM / MUTED TURQUOISE
PALE MINT
VERMILION RED
CHARCOAL BLACK
DIRTY WHITE

RED represents HUMAN LIFE.

Therefore:

all humans = red.

Never use red decoratively elsewhere,
except tiny mechanical warning indicators.

This color hierarchy must remain consistent.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PRINT TEXTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Maintain visible:

coarse halftone dots,
paper fibers,
screen-print grain,
slightly imperfect ink coverage,
subtle color misregistration,
rough analog edges.

Do not let motion remove the texture.

The platform especially should have
dense black halftone patterns.

The red figures should look
screen-printed onto the frame.

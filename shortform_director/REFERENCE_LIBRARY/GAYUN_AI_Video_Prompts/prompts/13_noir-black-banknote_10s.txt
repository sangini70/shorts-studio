Create a sophisticated short narrative animation about a mysterious businessman counting money alone late at night, until he discovers that someone has secretly entered the room.

Stylized hand-drawn 2D neo-noir animation.

STORY:

0.0–2.5 sec — MONEY

MACRO CLOSE-UP.

Stacks of banknotes fill the foreground of a wide 16:9 composition.

His enormous hands rapidly count money beneath the red lamp.

Bills flick rhythmically through his fingers.

CAMERA:
slow lateral dolly across the desk,
passing multiple stacks of cash.

The businessman remains mostly outside the frame.

AUDIO:
rapid rhythmic paper flicking:

frrt—frrt—frrt—frrt—

quiet clock ticking somewhere in the room.

A minimal noir soundtrack begins:
slow upright bass,
very soft brushed drums,
one distant piano note.


2.5–4.3 sec — THE MAN

HARD CUT.

Low-angle medium close-up.

The red lamp fills the left foreground.

The businessman occupies the right side of the cinematic widescreen composition.

Half his face is illuminated red.
Half disappears into black shadow.

His hands continue counting below frame.

CAMERA:
extremely slow push toward his face.

His expression is completely emotionless.

Then—

his fingers suddenly stop.

AUDIO:

money flicking abruptly stops.

Music stops with it.

Only:

tick.

tick.

tick.


4.3–6.2 sec — THE WRONG BILL

SMASH CUT.

EXTREME MACRO CLOSE-UP.

A single banknote between his fingers.

Among the normal money is ONE strange matte-black banknote.

He slowly pulls it from the stack.

Printed in its center:

a minimal mysterious eye symbol.

Nothing else.

The red lamp flickers.

Once.

Twice.

His thumb slowly moves across the strange symbol.

AUDIO:

electrical buzz:

bzzzt...

bzzzt...

Then—

deep restrained bass impact:

BOOM.


6.2–7.8 sec — HIS FACE

HARD CUT.

EXTREME CLOSE-UP OF HIS EYES.

For the first time,
his confidence disappears.

His eyes remain fixed on the black banknote.

Then his eyes slowly move upward.

Not toward the camera—

toward something BEHIND the camera.

His eyebrows tighten.

CAMERA:
very slow push closer.

AUDIO:

complete silence.

Then one faint wooden FLOOR CREAK behind him.

CREEEAK.


7.8–10.0 sec — THE REVEAL

HARD CUT.

WIDE OVER-THE-SHOULDER SHOT.

Use the full horizontal 16:9 frame.

Foreground:
the businessman sitting at the desk,
black banknote still between his fingers.

Center:
the glowing red hanging lamp.

Background:
a long dark room stretching far behind him.

At the far end of the room...

a tall human silhouette is standing perfectly still.

It was apparently there the entire time.

Hold this composition.

Nobody moves.

0.5 second uncomfortable silence.

The silhouette quietly says in a calm low male voice:

"You found it."

The businessman slowly turns his head.

Before we see his reaction—

THE RED LAMP SUDDENLY SWITCHES OFF.

CLICK.

PURE BLACK.

One final deep sub-bass:

THUMM.

END.

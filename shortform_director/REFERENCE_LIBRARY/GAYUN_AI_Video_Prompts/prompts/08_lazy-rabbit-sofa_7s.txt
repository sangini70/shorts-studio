Create a 7-second photorealistic comedic lifestyle video featuring a fluffy white rabbit relaxing like a lazy human on a sofa.

CORE CONCEPT:

A fluffy white rabbit wearing tiny black sunglasses is sprawled comfortably across a teal-blue sofa like someone spending a lazy night at home.

The rabbit holds a small TV remote.

A small pizza rests on its belly.
A glass of dark soda sits safely beside it.

The rabbit looks completely relaxed, bored, and slightly cool.

It lazily changes the TV channel.

For a moment, nothing happens.

Then it hears an exciting sports broadcast from the television.

Its ears suddenly shoot upright.

It slowly lowers its sunglasses with one paw and stares intensely toward the TV.

That is the entire joke.

Keep the story extremely simple.

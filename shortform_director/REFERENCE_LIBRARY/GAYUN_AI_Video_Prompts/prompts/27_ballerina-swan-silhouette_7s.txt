Create a 7-second artistic silhouette animation inspired by vintage theatrical poster art, shadow puppetry, ballet, and elegant optical illusion.

CORE IDEA:

A graceful ballerina and a swan exist inside the same visual composition.

At first their silhouettes overlap so perfectly that the ballerina's curved arm and body appear to form part of the swan.

As the ballerina slowly dances, the two shapes separate.

For one beautiful moment we realize:

there was both a dancer AND a swan hidden inside the same silhouette.

Then they elegantly merge again into one final graphic image.

Simple.
Poetic.
Surreal.
Elegant.

Only THREE major visual beats.

SHOT 1 — ONE SHAPE

STATIC WIDE COMPOSITION.

Midnight navy background.

A huge perfectly circular white moon
occupies the center of the frame.

In front of the moon:

a graceful ballerina silhouette
and a large swan silhouette overlap.

At first glance,
they appear to be ONE surreal creature.

The ballerina stands en pointe.

One arm curves elegantly upward.

The swan's long neck follows
almost exactly the same curve.

The dancer's blue skirt gently connects
with the blue water beneath her.

Nothing moves for the first half-second.

Then—

the ballerina slowly raises one arm.

Her wrist bends delicately.

Her fingers unfold.

The swan simultaneously
and very slowly turns its head.

The movement reveals that
they are actually two separate figures.

AUDIO:

Almost complete silence at first.

Very soft room-like theatrical ambience.

Then a single delicate piano note.

A faint sound of fabric:

fffff...

SHOT 2 — THE DANCE

WITHOUT A HARD CUT,
the composition begins moving.

The ballerina slowly rotates.

One elegant pirouette.

Her blue skirt expands
into a perfect circular graphic shape.

As she spins,
the skirt transforms visually
into a sweeping ultramarine brush-like ribbon.

The blue ribbon flows around her body.

The swan glides behind her
in the opposite direction.

Their silhouettes repeatedly overlap:

woman...

swan...

woman...

swan...

creating beautiful optical illusions
for fractions of a second.

The camera remains mostly fixed.

Do not create complicated camera movement.

Let the silhouettes create the motion.

The huge white moon remains perfectly still.

AUDIO:

Minimal solo piano melody begins.

Only a few delicate notes.

Soft ballet shoe contact:

tap...

Very subtle cloth movement.

As the dancer rotates:

soft airy WHOOSH.

No cinematic impact sounds.

No dialogue.

SHOT 3 — BECOME ONE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The dancer finishes the rotation
and extends one leg backward
into a beautiful arabesque.

Her arms stretch forward.

At exactly the same moment,

the swan raises its neck.

The dancer slowly moves behind the swan.

Their two silhouettes align.

Perfectly.

Her extended arm visually becomes
the beginning of the swan's neck.

Her torso disappears into its body.

Her blue skirt becomes
a small blue reflection beneath them.

For one moment—

the dancer completely disappears.

Only ONE elegant swan silhouette remains
inside the enormous white moon.

Hold.

Then the swan slowly lowers its head.

The blue water at the bottom
creates one tiny ripple.

CUT TO BLACK.

Slow,

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CRITICAL TRANSFORMATION RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do NOT use liquid morphing.

Do NOT visibly mutate the woman into an animal.

The transformation is created ONLY
through composition and overlapping silhouettes.

The ballerina remains a ballerina.

The swan remains a swan.

Their shapes simply align so precisely
that the viewer temporarily perceives them
as one object.

This optical illusion is the central visual idea.

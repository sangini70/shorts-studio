7-SECOND CINEMATIC STORY ANIMATION, 16:9 SQUARE.

Create a short surreal narrative animation based on a quiet UFO abducting a cow at night.

VISUAL STYLE:
Maintain one consistent vintage editorial illustration style throughout the entire video.
Mid-century surreal poster illustration, simplified geometric forms, muted dusty teal night sky, charcoal-black landscape, pale desaturated mint light, off-white cow with simple black patches.
Heavy analog paper grain, screen-print texture, subtle stippling, slightly imperfect ink edges, flat graphic shapes, minimal shading.
Quiet, mysterious, slightly humorous atmosphere.

Do NOT turn the scene photorealistic or 3D.
Everything must look like the same printed illustration coming alive.

STORY AND SHOT TRANSITIONS:

0.0–1.7 sec — THE QUIET FIELD

Wide establishing shot.

A single small cow stands alone in a huge empty dark field at night.
Distant geometric mountains overlap on the horizon.
The cow slowly chews grass.

Nothing unusual happens at first.

The camera very slowly pushes toward the cow.

AUDIO:
soft nighttime wind,
very distant insects,
subtle cow chewing,
almost complete silence.

Then a faint low-frequency electronic hum begins.

1.7–3.1 sec — SOMETHING ABOVE

HARD CUT.

Close-up of the cow's face from slightly below.

The cow suddenly stops chewing.

Its ears twitch.

Its eyes slowly look upward.

A strange pale mint light begins illuminating the top of its face.

The electronic hum grows louder.

The cow gives one confused:

"moo?"

CUT immediately on the moo.

3.1–5.3 sec — THE ABDUCTION

SMASH CUT TO EXTREME WIDE SHOT.

A huge dark flying saucer is now hovering directly above the tiny cow.

A perfectly geometric cone of pale mint-white light suddenly switches on.

WHUMM.

The cow's feet slowly leave the ground.

Grass and tiny pieces of dust rise upward around it.

The cow floats vertically toward the UFO,
legs dangling awkwardly beneath its body.

Camera tilts upward following the cow.

AUDIO:
deep retro sci-fi electrical drone,
soft rising synth tone,
wind suddenly pulling upward,
tiny frightened:

"MOOOO—"

The moo rises in pitch as the cow ascends.

5.3–7.0 sec — COMEDIC REVEAL

HARD CUT.

Interior of the UFO.

Completely different framing but EXACT SAME illustration style.

The cow gently drops onto the metallic floor.

Silence.

The cow looks forward.

REVERSE SHOT:

Three tiny grey aliens are sitting behind a simple table.

They are calmly staring at the cow.

One alien slowly holds up a small glass of milk.

Nobody moves.

Awkward pause.

The cow stares at the milk.

The alien stares at the cow.

AUDIO:

electronic hum suddenly stops.

0.5 second complete silence.

Tiny glass:
"clink."

The cow quietly says:

"…moo."

CUT TO BLACK.

STYLE CONSISTENCY:
Keep the same cow design across every shot.
Keep the UFO design consistent.
Limited palette: dusty teal, charcoal black, pale mint, dirty white and muted grey.
Strong negative space.
Flat geometric silhouettes.
Visible paper grain and vintage print texture in EVERY frame.
Slight frame-to-frame handmade animation texture is desirable.
Movement should feel deliberately simple, elegant and graphic.

EDITING:
Use obvious cinematic shot changes rather than morphing.
Wide shot → facial close-up → extreme wide abduction → UFO interior/reverse shot.
Cuts should feel intentional and synchronized with sound.
Allow brief pauses for comedy.
Do not overload the seven seconds with additional events.

NO typography.
NO subtitles.
NO logos.
NO watermark.
NO photorealism.
NO glossy CGI.
NO modern spaceship interface.
NO random camera movement.
NO character morphing.

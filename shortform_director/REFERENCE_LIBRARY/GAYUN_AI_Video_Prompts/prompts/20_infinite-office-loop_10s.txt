Create a 10-second surreal retro-corporate editorial animation.

CORE CONCEPT:

An enormous office containing hundreds of identical cubicles
extends infinitely into the distance.

Employees sit silently at desks,
typing, answering telephones and staring at old computer monitors.

The camera gradually discovers something impossible:

every computer monitor contains ANOTHER version
of the exact same office.

The camera enters one monitor,
revealing another office,
then another,
then another.

An infinite corporate loop.

At the very end,
one employee suddenly turns around
and looks directly at the viewer.

Then every employee turns around at once.

CUT.

Create a 10-second cinematic cyberpunk animated short film.

STORY:
In a gigantic neon megacity during a violent rainstorm,
a young man is falling from the top of a skyscraper.

At first it appears that he has lost control.

As he falls between enormous holographic billboards,
we see his face.

He is strangely calm.

A mysterious female voice speaks through his earpiece:

"Three seconds."

He looks toward the street thousands of feet below.

Instead of panicking, he smiles.

At the final moment, he activates something in his hand.

Gravity suddenly reverses.

He shoots upward.

CUT TO BLACK.

CINEMATOGRAPHY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This MUST feel like a rapidly edited cinematic sequence,
NOT one continuous falling shot.

Use:

extreme low-angle establishing shot,
falling facial close-up,
first-person POV,
extreme wide scale shot,
hand macro,
reaction close-up,
gravity-reversal wide shot,
fast upward tracking shot,
rooftop medium shot,
final character close-up.

Strong perspective distortion.

Use very wide-angle lenses for city shots.

Skyscrapers should dramatically converge toward the sky.

Human characters should frequently appear tiny
against gigantic architecture.

Use hard cuts and motivated match cuts.

Occasional fast camera rotation during falling sequences.

Do NOT constantly shake the camera.

Character close-ups should become comparatively stable,
allowing the audience to clearly read expressions.
